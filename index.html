75 years of faithfulness

Where the Free Presbyterian Church of Ulster began

On 17th March 1951, out of a mission in which 94 souls professed faith in Christ, Crossgar Free Presbyterian Church was constituted — the first congregation of a witness that would spread across the Province and around the world.
