New to Crossgar

You will be made very welcome.

Coming to a church for the first time can feel daunting. Here is everything you need to know before you visit — and a warm welcome from our minister.

A word of welcome

From our minister, Rev. Raymond Morrow

We would like to extend a warm welcome and a personal invitation to you and your family to come and join us as we worship the Lord Jesus Christ each week and learn from the Word of God.

Above all, we preach Christ crucified — proclaiming Jesus Christ as the only way of salvation. If we can be of any spiritual assistance to you, we would be only too glad to help.

What to expect

Your first Sunday

When to arrive

Doors open 15 mins early
Sundays

Morning worship begins at 11:30am and evening worship at 7:00pm. Arrive a little early and someone will help you find a seat.

What happens

Singing, prayer & preaching
Every service

We sing psalms and hymns, pray together, and hear the Bible faithfully preached. We use the King James Version.

Bringing children

Sabbath School 10:15am
Sept – May

Children are always welcome in the service, and there is Sabbath School and a Children’s Meeting through the year.

The heart of the matter

The ABC of Salvation

A

All have sinned and come short of the glory of God.

Romans 3 v 23
B

Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ and thou shalt be saved.

Acts 16 v 31
C

Come unto Me all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.

Matthew 11 v 28

May you put your faith in Him today and start out for Heaven.

We’d love to see you on Sunday.

Morning worship at 11:30am and evening worship at 7:00pm. No need to book — simply come.
